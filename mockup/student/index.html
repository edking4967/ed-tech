Friction & coefficient of friction
A Jun 5
Question: Explain the difference between static and kinetic friction with a practical example.
Your answer Static friction acts when an object is at rest — e.g., a book on a tilted table. Kinetic friction is lower and acts during motion. That's why it's harder to get something moving than to keep it moving.
Conservation of momentum
B+ Jun 4
Question: Describe what happens to momentum in a perfectly elastic collision.
Your answer In an elastic collision, total momentum and kinetic energy are both conserved. The objects bounce off each other without any energy lost to heat or deformation.
Free body diagrams

Question: Draw and label the forces on a block resting on an inclined plane.
Your answer Weight (mg) acts straight down, normal force perpendicular to the surface, and friction opposing potential sliding. Weight splits into components parallel and perpendicular to the plane.
